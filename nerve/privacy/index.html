Privacy and data flow

Free Alpha

Two different things get confused constantly, so this page keeps them apart: what the Nerve product sends to an AI provider during play, and what this website collects when you visit it or send us feedback.

Part one — the Nerve product

The headline

The Nerve bridge runs on your own machine, but Nerve is not an offline product, and we will not tell you your data never leaves your computer. Selected campaign context and source references are sent out to the external AI provider you choose.

What gets sent, and to whom

When you make a request during a tracked session, the extension gathers relevant campaign context, the local companion passes it to the AI application you selected, and that provider processes it on their own systems under your own account. Backchannel Studios is not in the middle of that exchange and does not receive your campaign content.

GM-only material can be included

This is the part most groups do not expect. "GM-only" governs what players see inside Fantasy Grounds. It does not stop material from being sent to the provider. Secrets, notes and unrevealed plot can be part of the context a request carries.

Your provider's terms apply, not ours

  • Your provider decides how long they retain what you send, and who at that provider can see it. Read their policy — it governs your campaign content, not anything we publish.
  • Your AI account costs and allowances are separate from Nerve. Tests and gameplay both consume your allowance. Nerve is free during alpha; your provider is not necessarily.
  • Sign-in is owned by your provider. Nerve does not see or store your provider password.

Get your group's agreement first

Campaign context is not only yours — it includes your players' characters, their decisions and things they said at the table. Before you route a campaign through an AI provider, tell your group which provider you are using and let them read its policy. That is a conversation to have before session one, not after.

Part two — this website

Separate from anything the product does. This section is about thebackchannel.studio itself.

Browsing these pages

  • No analytics and no marketing scripts. There is no tracking script, no advertising pixel and no third-party analytics on this site. Downloads and setup instructions work with scripts blocked entirely.
  • No cookies are set by this site, and nothing here asks for consent to set any.
  • Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. That is the only third-party request these pages make.
  • Standard hosting logs. The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps ordinary server request logs as part of running the service.

Sending a feedback report

  • Only what you type. The form collects the fields on it and nothing else. Every field except category, summary and description is optional, and an email address is only needed if you ask for a reply.
  • Text only. The form accepts no file uploads, no diagnostic bundles and no campaign archives, by design.
  • Spam protection without a CAPTCHA. A hidden field and a form-fill timing check, plus rate limiting. To rate-limit, the server briefly holds your IP address in memory; it is not written to a database by us and it is not attached to your report.
  • Reports are not published. What you send is not posted publicly by us.
Please keep these out of a report

Passwords, API keys and tokens; private conversations with your players; text from licensed adventures; and spoilers your group has not reached. If we need a support bundle, we will ask for it and take it privately — review it yourself before sending it anywhere.

Still pending owner approval

Owner input required before publication

The following have not been decided yet, and this page is not going to invent them. Until they are settled and this section is replaced with approved wording, the feedback endpoint is disabled and returns an error rather than quietly accepting reports:

  • Where feedback reports are stored and which service holds them.
  • Who can read them.
  • How long they are kept, and what happens at the end of that period.
  • How to ask for your report to be deleted, and where to send that request.
  • The identity of the data controller and the contact address for privacy questions.

We are deliberately not publishing a retention period or a response-time promise that has not been committed to. In the meantime you can reach us at backchannelstudios1@gmail.com.
