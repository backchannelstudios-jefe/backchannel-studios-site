Tabletop Middleware / Apps

Nerve Free Alpha

Nerve for Fantasy Grounds →

A local AI-to-application middleware platform designed to give AI systems structured, permissioned access during live tabletop RPG sessions and virtual tabletops like Fantasy Grounds Unity.

  • Real-time campaign observation and session continuity
  • Secure local architecture with extensible adapters
  • Automated DM tooling and rulebook integration
  • Guarded actions that need GM approval before they change campaign records

The Fantasy Grounds alpha is free while it is being tested. It needs a Windows GM host, the 5E ruleset, the Nerve companion, Node.js 22 or newer, and your own compatible AI account — AI access is not included. Requirements, setup and downloads →

Mobile & Tabletop Game

Brush Pass Closed Alpha

Brush Pass for Android →

A fast tactical bluffing game of Cold War espionage. Eight operatives who work for whoever has secretly paid them the most, one briefcase, four rival services, and a challenge system where every claim can be a lie — as long as it is an understatement.

  • Solo against AI opponents, or online at a table of four
  • League ratings, global rankings and an interactive tutorial
  • Cosmetic operative packs and character dossiers
  • In closed testing on Google Play — volunteer to playtest →
