      </ul>

      <h3>This is alpha software</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Setup and gameplay behaviour are still being tested. Provider and multiplayer coverage are uneven and are not certified.</li>
        <li>Nerve is not a fully autonomous or fully reliable replacement for a game master, and is not presented as one.</li>
        <li>Nerve can change campaign records through supported actions. Back up your campaign and test on a copy.</li>
        <li>Known limitations are published openly on the <a href="/nerve#limitations">overview</a> and <a href="/nerve/releases">release notes</a>.</li>
      </ul>

      <h3>What you bring, and what you are billed for</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Nerve requires a compatible external AI application and your own account with that provider.</li>
        <li>AI access, allowance and costs are not included with Nerve. Your provider bills you directly; we do not meter, cap or pay for provider usage.</li>
