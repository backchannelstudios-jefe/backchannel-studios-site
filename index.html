About

An independent studio built for cross-disciplinary work.

Backchannel Studios is an independent studio and production house. We move between disciplines by design — the same team that ships an indie tabletop app also builds practical effects for a live stage production.

That range shows up in how we work: tight, permissioned software for the table, and tangible, cinematic craft for the screen and stage. Whether the deliverable is a build, a rig, or a compositing pass, we hold it to the same bar.
